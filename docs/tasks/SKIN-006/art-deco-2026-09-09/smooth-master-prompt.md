Create a newly rendered, high-quality Art Deco Agon Jukebox interface. The supplied image is a reference for the composition, ornament, and material colors ONLY. Redraw the design from scratch as polished, smooth, high-resolution illustration suitable as a master image for later downsampling. Do not preserve the reference's coarse pixel grid or bitmap lettering.

Output: one full-resolution 4:3 landscape PNG, ideally 2048x1536 or the highest available native 4:3 resolution. Full continuous RGB color and smooth antialiasing. Keep this image high resolution; no low-resolution conversion or palette quantization yet.

Visual treatment: elegant 1920s/1930s Art Deco gold and brass metalwork, dark navy-black enamel panels, luminous cream-gold glass columns, deep ruby jewels and restrained turquoise inlays. Crisp professionally drawn contours, continuous smooth curves, carefully beveled metal edges, refined natural metallic shading and subtle highlights. Symmetrical cabinet architecture with fan ornaments and stepped architectural moldings. Those large structural steps belong to Art Deco; individual diagonal and curved edges must remain perfectly smooth. Flat straight-on orthographic interface filling the entire canvas, without photographic perspective or a surrounding room. Preserve clear readable panel interiors, strong bilateral borders, and the reference's balanced layout.

Typography is especially important: smooth, well-designed high-resolution lettering with clean curves and antialiased edges, never a pixel font. Heading "AGON JUKEBOX" in a handsome geometric Art Deco display face. Table labels and filenames in a clear medium-weight monospaced typeface, with sufficient stroke weight and spacing for later reduction. No ragged square pixels on any letter.
